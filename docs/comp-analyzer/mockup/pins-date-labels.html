Comp pins — saying which date is which, without eating the map

Live pills stay unlabelled (a 39d next to a 77d is what shipped). Words on the pill do not fit — “39d ago” / “took 77d” measured ~15–40px extra, which is the whole two-line layout. These four encodings try to make the two numbers different kinds of thing, so they don’t need a caption.

Real Detroit board, 12 of the 50, same colours and fade.
